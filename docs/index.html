Follower Mosaic

粉丝拼图 · Turn your X followers' avatars into one beautiful image.

Privacy policy · 隐私政策 Source on GitHub Made by @Kuno_he

What it does

A Chrome extension: open x.com, click the icon, press one button. It reads the follower list slowly and read-only inside your own logged-in tab, then draws the avatars as your name, a galaxy, a heart or a grid. No password, no OAuth, no server; the image is made on your computer.

这是什么

一个 Chrome 插件：打开 x.com，点插件图标，按一个按钮。它在你自己已登录的页面里慢慢地、只读地读取粉丝列表，把头像拼成你的昵称、星系、爱心或头像墙。不要密码、不用授权、没有服务器，图片在你的电脑上生成。
