Privacy Policy

Follower Mosaic 粉丝拼图 · Chrome extension · Effective 2026-10-20

Summary

We don't collect, store on our servers, sell or share any of your data. We don't run any servers. Everything the extension does happens inside your own browser.

What the extension accesses

  • Follower list (public profile info): display names, @usernames and profile-picture URLs of the account you choose and its followers. This is read from x.com when you click the button, only to draw the image.
  • Profile pictures: loaded from pbs.twimg.com and drawn onto an image in your browser.
  • Your existing x.com session: the requests run inside your open x.com tab and use the session you already have, exactly as x.com's own website does. The extension never asks for, sees or stores your password. It does not use OAuth or API keys.

How it is used and stored

  • Used only to generate the mosaic image you asked for.
  • The follower list exists only in the memory of your x.com tab while the extension's panel is open, and is discarded when you close it. Only small preferences (interface language, font, custom text) are saved in chrome.storage.local on your computer.
  • The image is saved to your computer only when you click "Download".

What we never do

  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. There are no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • No posting, following, liking, messaging or any other change to any account. All requests are read-only.
  • No selling or transferring of data, and no use for purposes unrelated to making the image.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting run the extension's bundled helper in the x.com tab, only after you click the toolbar button. Host access to x.com / twitter.com lets a tiny script note the follower-list requests x.com itself makes (kept in the tab's memory only, nothing is sent anywhere) and re-open the panel if it had to reload the page once. storage keeps small preferences (language, font, custom text, reading speed, follower limit) locally. No other website is accessed.
